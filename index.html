About

Arie Savvy, whose real name is Kabelo Selomo, was born on August 10, 2001, in Botha-Bothe, Lesotho. He is a soulful deep house producer and pop artist blending African rhythm with modern house grooves. Arie began his music career in 2021, producing deep house and recording music that takes listeners on a journey of emotion, culture, and creativity.

He is currently in an exclusive relationship with Pontsho Motsoasele since September 4, 2024.
