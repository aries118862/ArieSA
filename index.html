About

Arie Savvy, born Kabelo Selomo on August 10, 2001, in Botha-Bothe, Lesotho, is a soulful deep house producer and pop artist blending African rhythm with modern house grooves.

He began his career in 2021, creating music that takes listeners on a journey of emotion, culture, and creativity.

Arie is currently in an exclusive relationship with Pontsho Motsoasele since September 4, 2024.
