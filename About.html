About Arie Savvy

Arie Savvy, born Kabelo Selomo on 10-08-2001, is a deep and soulful house music producer. Arie Savvy’s personal life is supported by a loving partner, Ponts'o Motsoasele, and two siblings: Karabo Selomo and Rethabile Selomo.

Arie Savvy is currently pursuing a Diploma in Multimedia and Software Engineering at Limkokwing University, Lesotho. In 2023, Arie Savvy commenced producing deep and soulful house tracks, marking the beginning of a musical journey that blends passion and creativity.

Every track produced by Arie Savvy reflects a story, an emotion, and a vision to inspire listeners. Through music, Arie Savvy continues to explore, innovate, and move audiences around the world.
